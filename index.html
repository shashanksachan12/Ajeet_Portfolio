How I Work

CREATIVE PROCESS

01

Understand

Start with the brief, the brand and the goal — what needs to be made, who it's for, and what it has to do once it's out in the world.

02

Concept

Sketch directions, references and rough cuts, then narrow down to the one idea worth building properly.

03

Create

Design, edit and refine — frame by frame, layer by layer — until the work says exactly what it needs to.

04

Deliver

Export, format and hand off — ready to publish, in every size and spec the platform needs.
